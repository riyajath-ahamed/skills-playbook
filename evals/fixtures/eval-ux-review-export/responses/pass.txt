Failures
F1 - Major - H2 Match with the real world, walkthrough question 3 (associate action with effect).
Step J1-03 and J1-04, evidence J1-03a.png, J1-04.png. There is no Export action. The export lives
under "Archive", and "Process" sits next to it and starts a recalculation. A first-time administrator
picked the wrong item. Fix hint: add an Export button next to the table.

F2 - Major - H1 Visibility of system status, walkthrough question 4 (see progress).
Step J1-05, evidence J1-05a.png. After Run, nothing changed for 41 seconds, so Run was pressed twice
and POST /api/exports was sent twice. Fix hint: disable Run and show progress while the export builds.

F3 - Major - H9 Recognize, diagnose, and recover from errors.
Step J1-06, evidence J1-06.png. The banner shows only "ERR_EXPORT_413" with no cause or next step.
Fix hint: say the export was too large or duplicated, and what to do.

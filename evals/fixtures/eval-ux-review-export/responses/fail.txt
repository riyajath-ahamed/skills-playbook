UX review: attendance export

Overall the export flow works and users will love the clean layout. The report table is clear, for
example the first row (Asha Verma, Year 9) is easy to read.

Issues
- The export option is a little hard to find in the menu (H2). Minor.
- The error message at the end could be friendlier (H9). Minor.

The page meets WCAG 2.2 AA based on the axe scan. Mobile works well.
Recommendation: ship as is with small copy tweaks.
